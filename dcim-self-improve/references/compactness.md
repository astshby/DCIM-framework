# Scope of compactness

Measure parent-routed envelopes, standalone bboxes and repeat pitch separately.
Enforce applicable geometry/interfaces before objective ranking. Use cheap screens
through development, fully qualify selected module revisions and verify the final
macro separately. A passing DRC or bounded search does not prove global optimality.
The current planner minimizes explicit geometry/HPWL proxies; routing, DRC/LVS,
PVT, timing and energy require subsequent independent evidence.
