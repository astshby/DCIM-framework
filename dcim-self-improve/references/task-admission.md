# Area-Driven Task Admission

Read at structural choices, hierarchy advancement and requests for costly
qualification. Revision: area-admission-20261102. Applies prospectively;
do not relabel old candidates or alter an active packet without explicit handoff.

## Structural search

Default to two mechanism-distinct candidates on the same function, hierarchy,
H/W/interface and load boundary. Each names the cost it could recover, causal
mechanism, evidence, risks, cheapest decisive screen, stop criterion and cost
estimate or unknown. Pitch, coordinate and router-order variants of one
construction are not independent mechanisms.

Actually screen both before selecting expensive implementation when inputs
support doing so. Suitable screens include original source/view inspection,
rule-bound placement, critical-access witnesses and small repeat/shared-boundary
probes. Use original local PV when simplified geometry cannot settle the question.
Screens do not qualify a whole design; no blanket PEX/post for both candidates.
Run the two low-cost screens concurrently with separate Builders, the same frozen
baseline and disjoint output/job/OA namespaces, within aggregate authorized
resources. Neither Builder waits for or consumes the other's result. Analyst
compares both terminal outcomes, including explicit failed/unavailable outcomes.
If concurrency is infeasible, record the resource/input-dependency reason for
serial execution; this does not authorize two costly implementation branches.

A single-route exception must identify why the alternative is inadmissible,
redundant, unavailable or not worth its measured/estimated cost. A diagnosed
ordinary repair may use one route without becoming a structural search. Do not
invent a competitor or claim superiority over a deferred/untested route.

## Macro-area account

Analyst maintains a versioned-in-run `area_account.md` (or an existing structured
equivalent) and links its current revision from every design decision. State:

- Full target and comparison boundary, units, topology-derived repetition counts.
- Current accepted physical and electrical baselines, separately.
- Contribution by storage, access/LRCC, arithmetic, context/taps, parent routing,
  decode/drivers and other applicable periphery; avoid double-counting overlaps.
- Measured values, projections, bounded estimates and unknowns, separately.
  Unknown costs and sharing benefits are not zero. Independent bbox sums are
  screens, not measured macro area or strict bounds after repacking/sharing.
- Remaining target gap, dominant recoverable costs, candidate net benefit and
  added wire/buffer/PG costs. Give evidence for ranges; do not invent confidence.
- Untried freedoms such as folding, legal sizing, cell choice and context sharing;
  why the selected action outranks testing the promising ones first.

An incomplete account is still useful: expose the missing quantity and select
the cheapest observation that could change the decision. Do not turn a zero-other-
cost division into a hard leaf contract. A qualified baseline can be retained
even when it is not credible for unchanged macro replication.

## Separate acceptance from admission

Report three independent judgments: correctness/qualification, local usability,
and whether broader implementation is worth its cost. Classify the next task:

| Tier | Required justification |
| --- | --- |
| repair | Diagnosed failure and bounded affected regression on the current block |
| screen | Competing mechanism or missing quantity with a decisive cheap test |
| compact | Recoverable cost and meaningful same-boundary physical trial |
| parent-observation | Specific unknown requiring a minimal parent/repeat witness |
| expand | Evidence-supported macro-area route plus justified cost of the larger block |
| qualify | A selected valuable candidate and the unresolved qualification gates |

When the current implementation's replication projection already overshoots the
whole target before missing costs, full expansion is not the default. Require
measured or rule/view-supported net savings that plausibly address the gap and
leave room for missing components, or first choose `screen`, `compact` or
`parent-observation`. Do not declare global infeasibility from that projection.

A parent-observation packet specifies the unknown, hypotheses, smallest witness,
cost ceiling/resource scope, result that changes the next action, and stop point.
It does not automatically grow into a full tile plus OA/PEX/post. An unknown
cost ceiling must trigger a cheap cost-estimation step before costly dispatch.
Do not use generic "information value" or lack of leaf optimality as an exemption.

Physical invalidity blocks ordinary PEX/post; insufficient area value blocks
automatic broad qualification, not cheap functional baselines necessary to
evaluate a changed independent circuit. Final acceptance requirements stay intact.

## Dispatch responsibility

Analyst supplies the account, actual route-screen states, tier, untested-freedom
review and stop/return boundary. Main chat checks these before launching Builder,
including requested stages against tier and resource authorization. Missing
fields or unsupported expansion return for a bounded correction; they are not
silently waived by the next-stage checklist or a successful local DRC/LVS.

Do not create an extra scoring role or weighted total score to implement this
gate. Safety/function/rules cannot be traded away by an area or information score.
