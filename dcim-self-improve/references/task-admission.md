# Task admission and area accounting

Main checks each proposed task against architecture, applicable MUST constraints,
execution authorization and resource limits. Analyst supplies its basis from
actual evidence. A bounded task delivers a connected block or decisive experiment.
Ordinary in-contract debugging stays with Builder until the declared boundary.

At structural decisions identify meaningful alternatives and the cheapest
observation that can change the decision. Select one or more actual screens
when justified by uncertainty and budget. Parallel execution is optional and
requires authorized disjoint outputs/job namespaces and aggregate resources.
Routine diagnosed repair needs no artificial competitor or new mechanism.
An untested or deferred route retains its uncertainty.

## Macro-area account

For area-driven composition maintain the current target/comparison boundary,
units, source-derived repetition counts and physical/electrical baseline scopes.
Separate measured storage/compute/periphery/context/routing costs, projections,
sharing hypotheses and unknowns. Unknown costs remain unknown. Independent bbox
sums cannot establish the joined routed macro area or savings after sharing.

State the target gap, recoverable costs, added access/buffer/PG costs and relevant
untried freedoms. Record support for bounds. Hard parent W/H/function/interface
conditions apply before area ranking. A local passing component can remain useful
while broader expansion lacks justification.

## Bounded execution tiers

Use `repair`, `screen`, `compact`, `parent-observation`, `expand` or `qualify` when
helpful. Match checks and resources to the admitted tier. A parent observation
names its unknown, smallest witness, decision-changing outcome, resource ceiling
and stop point. It cannot silently grow into a full macro qualification.

When unchanged replication already overshoots the complete target, investigate
plausible recoverable cost or bounded parent evidence before costly expansion.
An incomplete projection cannot establish global infeasibility. Preserve required
functional/electrical verification of a changed independent circuit. Cost and
area objectives never waive foundry or correctness checks.
