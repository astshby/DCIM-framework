# Decision Evidence Policy

Builder performs verification; Analyst makes concrete decisions from the concise
result, completed check outputs, area account and scoped experience. Main chat
checks task admission and dispatches. Analyst is retained, but is not a separate
mandatory evidence-replay reviewer.

Preserve the distinction between geometry, original DRC, independent-source LVS,
electrical behavior and whole-macro acceptance. A geometry screen is not foundry
qualification; resizing requires affected electrical checks. Keep those checks
even though duplicate agent review is removed.

Reuse check outputs bound to the exact frozen artifacts. For missing provenance,
conflicting measurements or changed dependencies, identify the exact gap and
request a targeted Builder check only if it can change the decision. A short
original excerpt can settle a question; do not replay whole evidence sets.
Record what actually ran and remains unverified. Do not claim independent
verification from merely reading Builder's outputs.

Analyst decides continue/switch/stop, writes the next executable task, applies
task-admission.md and updates route memory, area account and PROGRESS.md.
Use the Analyst decision template, not a mandatory audit packet. Preserve old
evidence and historical decisions. User-requested audits are separate work.
